## About me

I'm Cleiton José Osti, but you can call me **Osti**.

I'm a **Software Developer from Brazil** with a strong focus on **backend development using .NET and ASP.NET Core**.

Technology has been part of my life since I was 18 years old. Since then, I have been building my career around solving problems, designing systems, and continuously improving my technical skills.

My motto is: **"Never stop learning."**  
For me, studying is more than a necessity — it is part of who I am.

I have experience with **C#, .NET, ASP.NET Core, JavaScript, React, SQL Server, Oracle, Azure, and GCP**. Over time, I developed a strong interest in **Software Architecture, Cloud Solutions, Messaging, Microservices, API Design, Database Performance, and Scalable Systems**.

I believe programming languages are just tools. What really matters is understanding problems deeply and building solid, maintainable, and scalable solutions.
